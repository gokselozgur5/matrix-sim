# D-024 — Attention-graded fidelity

Status: 🔵 idea · Phase gate: v4.0 · Thread: #28

## Context
Nobody observes the whole world at once; simulating unwatched regions at full fidelity is waste.

## Decision
Parked: regions without connected minds degrade to statistics; fidelity follows attention.

## Consequences
Déjà vu events become the visible cost of re-materialization; D-018 budgets are the first step.
