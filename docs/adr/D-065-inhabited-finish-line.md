# D-065 — The film is the specimen; inhabited causality is the finish line

*In the context of evolving the completed deterministic film simulator under D-062, facing D-036's truthful but narrower declaration that reproducing the trilogy completes the project, we lean toward retaining the film as the completed Season One specimen while defining one deterministic ordinary non-hero day as the first Living Matrix foundation finish line, and against treating the film as the whole product, discarding it, or replacing it with a feature count, to preserve historical victory and demand inhabited causality, accepting a new counterfactual, persistence, social-response and replay proof.*

## Context and Problem Statement

D-036 gave the project the end it needed. One command reproduced the trilogy, the declared crowns closed and Season One became complete. D-062 later names a larger product claim: a resident is a causal subject only when situated experience changes accumulated interpretation, reachable intent, committed consequence, biography and future perception.

The two claims are compatible only if their scopes are named. The film proves that deterministic mechanisms can reproduce one canonical specimen. It does not prove that an ordinary resident can live a causally continuous life inside the system. Calling the first proof false would rewrite history; calling it sufficient would make D-062 ornamental.

What successor finish line is small enough to close, rich enough to prove inhabited causality, and strict enough that logs or scripted hero privilege cannot counterfeit it?

## Decision Drivers

* D-036's completed evidence and exact film profile must remain reproducible and quotable
* Film specimen, bounded season completion and current product completion must stop being ambiguous synonyms
* The first new witness must center an ordinary resident rather than The One, prophecy, pills or named-character privilege
* One declared percept-delivery split must be the first causal divergence between two otherwise identical histories
* A choice must carry a real competing cost and persist into biography, a reciprocal relationship and an institution
* The changed consequence must participate in later perception rather than terminate as narration
* Replay, observer isolation and complete causal attribution must make the witness falsifiable
* Old season evidence may be inherited without preserving the old story order as the current priority order

## Considered Options

* Keep D-036 as the whole product finish line
* Discard D-036 and replace the film proof
* Retain D-036 as the completed Season One specimen and define a successor inhabited-causality finish line
* Explicit alternative with an equally bounded subject, counterfactual, persistence, social response and replay proof

## Decision Outcome

Proposed option: "retain the film, move the finish line", because a completed proof should keep the claim it earned while a larger product must earn a larger proof. Final call in thread #1671 (phase gate).

The proposed successor is one deterministic ordinary non-hero day. A resident receives or lawfully does not receive the same friend's request for help; delivered evidence must travel through persistent mind state into a validated choice, a costly consequence, reciprocal and institutional change, and later perception. Both branches and the historical film profile must replay exactly.

### Consequences

* Good, because the old success becomes a regression foundation rather than a false universal claim
* Good, because “somebody lives here” becomes one bounded command-level proof
* Good, because the witness forces cognition, agency, relationships and institutions to meet in one vertical slice
* Bad, because the current roadmap's story order no longer decides what is built next
* Bad, because one day now needs stronger causal and persistence evidence than an entire scripted film run

### Confirmation

The accepted decision must define film specimen, season completion and Living Matrix foundation completion separately; add a reciprocal D-036 disposition; classify the inherited six-season order; and state the ordinary-day subject, single split, full D-062 loop, trade-off, persistence, social response, replay and observer-isolation proof.

The decision PR is documentation-only. DocLint must prove record/index/ROADMAP agreement; DocFigures and DocsRoster must remain green; daemon selftests and the digest leash must show no world-byte change. An adversarial review must try hero privilege, name-sheet fate, narrated-only friendship, costless virtue, pre-split divergence, observer causation and deleted-film counterexamples.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Keep D-036 as the whole product finish line

* Good, because completion remains one already-green command
* Neutral, because later work can still be called optional epilogue
* Bad, because D-062's subject contract becomes desirable prose rather than a product obligation
* Bad, because a scripted film arc can satisfy completion while no ordinary mind causes anything

### Discard D-036 and replace the film proof

* Good, because the roadmap would have one current finish line
* Neutral, because many film mechanisms may still survive as implementation details
* Bad, because it retroactively denies an honestly completed scope contract
* Bad, because deleting regression evidence makes the richer world easier to fake, not truer

### Retain the film and define an inhabited successor

* Good, because each proof keeps exactly the claim it can support
* Good, because a small counterfactual day joins every missing Human causal layer
* Good, because the historical profile becomes an independent regression obligation
* Bad, because two finish-line scopes must stay explicitly named in living canon

### Explicit alternative

* Good, because a different ordinary witness may prove the same causal obligations more cleanly
* Neutral, because scene details should remain realization choices
* Bad, because an alternative stated as breadth, class count or issue closure does not answer the causal question
