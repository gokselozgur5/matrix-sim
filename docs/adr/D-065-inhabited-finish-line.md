# D-065 — The film is the specimen; inhabited causality is the finish line

*In the context of evolving the completed deterministic film simulator under D-062, facing D-036's truthful but narrower declaration that reproducing the trilogy completes the project, we lean toward retaining the film as the completed Season One specimen while defining one deterministic ordinary non-hero day as the first Living Matrix foundation finish line, and against treating the film as the whole product, discarding it, or replacing it with a feature count, to preserve historical victory and demand inhabited causality, accepting a new counterfactual, persistence, social-response and replay proof.*

## Context and Problem Statement

D-036 gave the project the end it needed. One command reproduced the trilogy, the declared crowns closed and Season One became complete. D-062 later names a larger product claim: a resident is a causal subject only when situated experience changes accumulated interpretation, reachable intent, committed consequence, biography and future perception.

The two claims are compatible only if their scopes are named. The film proves that deterministic mechanisms can reproduce one canonical specimen. It does not prove that an ordinary resident can live a causally continuous life inside the system. Calling the first proof false would rewrite history; calling it sufficient would make D-062 ornamental.

What successor finish line is small enough to close, rich enough to prove inhabited causality, and strict enough that logs or scripted hero privilege cannot counterfeit it?

## Decision Drivers

* D-036's completed evidence and exact film profile must remain reproducible and quotable
* Film specimen, bounded season completion and current product completion must stop being ambiguous synonyms
* The first new witness must center an ordinary resident rather than The One, prophecy, pills or named-character privilege
* One declared percept-delivery split must be the first causal divergence between two otherwise identical histories
* A choice must carry a real competing cost and persist into biography, a reciprocal relationship and an institution
* The changed consequence must participate in later perception rather than terminate as narration
* Replay, observer isolation and complete causal attribution must make the witness falsifiable
* Old season evidence may be inherited without preserving the old story order as the current priority order

## Considered Options

* Keep D-036 as the whole product finish line
* Discard D-036 and replace the film proof
* Retain D-036 as the completed Season One specimen and define a successor inhabited-causality finish line
* Explicit alternative with an equally bounded subject, counterfactual, persistence, social response and replay proof

## Decision Outcome

Proposed option: "retain the film, move the finish line", because a completed proof should keep the claim it earned while a larger product must earn a larger proof. Final call in thread #1671 (phase gate).

The proposed successor is one deterministic ordinary non-hero day. A resident receives or lawfully does not receive the same friend's request for help; delivered evidence must travel through persistent mind state into a validated choice, a costly consequence, reciprocal and institutional change, and later perception. Both branches and the historical film profile must replay exactly.

### Consequences

* Good, because the old success becomes a regression foundation rather than a false universal claim
* Good, because “somebody lives here” becomes one bounded command-level proof
* Good, because the witness forces cognition, agency, relationships and institutions to meet in one vertical slice
* Bad, because the current roadmap's story order no longer decides what is built next
* Bad, because one day now needs stronger causal and persistence evidence than an entire scripted film run

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Keep D-036 as the whole product finish line

* Good, because completion remains one already-green command
* Neutral, because later work can still be called optional epilogue
* Bad, because D-062's subject contract becomes desirable prose rather than a product obligation
* Bad, because a scripted film arc can satisfy completion while no ordinary mind causes anything

### Discard D-036 and replace the film proof

* Good, because the roadmap would have one current finish line
* Neutral, because many film mechanisms may still survive as implementation details
* Bad, because it retroactively denies an honestly completed scope contract
* Bad, because deleting regression evidence makes the richer world easier to fake, not truer

### Retain the film and define an inhabited successor

* Good, because each proof keeps exactly the claim it can support
* Good, because a small counterfactual day joins every missing Human causal layer
* Good, because the historical profile becomes an independent regression obligation
* Bad, because two finish-line scopes must stay explicitly named in living canon

### Explicit alternative

* Good, because a different ordinary witness may prove the same causal obligations more cleanly
* Neutral, because scene details should remain realization choices
* Bad, because an alternative stated as breadth, class count or issue closure does not answer the causal question

## Accepted Outcome — Preserve the victory, move the finish line

The Architect accepted the retain-and-succeed option in thread #1671 on 2026-08-19 as part of the verdict “üçü de kabul.” This section records that verdict without deleting or rewriting the proposal it answered.

### Three scopes, three honest claims

* **Film specimen:** the historical seed/profile in which the deterministic trilogy remains reproducible. It proves its declared mechanisms and canon at that profile; it does not define the whole product ontology.
* **Season completion:** every declared child of one bounded season or gate is closed and its Confirmation holds. A season may be complete while the system remains open to a successor scope.
* **Living Matrix foundation completion:** one ordinary non-hero resident traverses the complete D-062 causal loop in a baseline and one lawful counterfactual, with durable social consequence and exact replay. This is a bounded successor finish line, not a claim that the world can never grow again.

D-036 therefore remains accepted as the completed Season One film specimen. Its 6,000-tick command, trilogy, crowns and budget evidence remain quotable at the pinned historical profile. Its sentence “the project is COMPLETE” remains true about the scope contract it closed in August 2026 and becomes historical when used as a claim about the D-062 product.

### The first inhabited-day witness

The canonical witness is deliberately mundane. A deterministically generated worker or citizen already has a reciprocal friend and belongs to a workplace institution. There is no One, prophecy, red pill, scripted rescue, named-character exemption or observer-authored fate.

The two runs share seed, input history, world truth and canonical state through the start of one declared perception-delivery split:

1. The friend emits the same request for help in both worlds.
2. In the **delivered** branch, a canonical delivery audit admits a percept receipt carrying only what the resident can perceive. In the **occluded** branch, the audit records a named delivery policy and no hidden-fact receipt enters the mind; an observable silence/no-signal receipt is allowed only if that silence itself is available to the resident.
3. “Lawful occlusion” means reproducible execution through a named rule, not moral or contractual innocence. The audit separately classifies authority, consent, deception, coercion and any D-064 breach/obligation.
4. Delivered evidence is interpreted against persistent memory of the friend and the reciprocal relationship. It changes a need/value tension, makes a goal reachable and proposes an intent to detour from the commute.
5. The composition root validates current capability, constraints, consent/authority and world truth. Intent mutates nothing; only a committed action may produce the detour.
6. The committed detour helps the friend and makes the resident late. The occluded branch preserves the ordinary commute and leaves the friend without that help. The witness therefore contains a real trade-off rather than costless virtue.
7. Biography records what the resident knew and did. Reciprocal trust changes through the attributable act. The workplace responds to lateness under its own policy and available knowledge rather than an omniscient moral narrator.
8. On a later tick, changed relationship and institutional state alter what the resident can perceive, expect, access or attempt. The loop closes on future causality rather than a terminal log line.

Scene names, exact occupations, travel geometry, dialogue, psychology scales and class layout remain realization choices. A different ordinary-day fixture is admissible only through a superseding decision or an equivalence argument proving every causal and evidential obligation above.

### Counterfactual and replay contract

* Each branch replays byte-identically from its declared inputs.
* A canonical diff proves the histories are identical before the delivery decision and identifies the delivery outcome as the first divergence.
* Every later Human-attributed effect traces through receipt → mind transition → goal/intent → validation/commit or explicit non-choice cause → consequence.
* The delivered branch cannot be reproduced by changing only a name, identity-derived sheet, probe, follow mode or observer setting.
* Removing prose, probes and observers leaves canonical state and digest unchanged.
* Biography, reciprocal relationship, institutional state and any D-064 obligation survive beyond the scene and participate in later causality.
* The historical film profile reproduces separately; the ordinary-day proof cannot edit the trilogy or its old ledger semantics to make itself pass.

### Roadmap disposition

* The six-season history remains an archive of completed evidence, accepted decisions and reusable deterministic substrate.
* Its old linear order is retired as the current implementation order. Human Foundation dependencies and the smallest ordinary-day vertical slice lead.
* Mechanisms are inherited only where they serve that slice without violating D-064/D-066.
* Story-specific Truce, mission, cameo and hero work is parked after the ordinary-day foundation proof.
* Feature count, class count, contribution count and issue-closure count cannot substitute for the witness.

### Accepted falsifiers

* A resident detours because a name-derived stat crosses a threshold without receiving and interpreting the call → fail
* Output narrates friendship but no persistent reciprocal state changes → fail
* Helping carries no competing cost or institutional response → fail
* The branches diverge before the declared delivery split → fail
* Occluded truth appears in belief or intent → fail
* An observer or probe changes digest or makes the branch happen → fail
* Later perception is unchanged because consequences never became causal → fail
* The film regression is deleted or silently rewritten rather than retained at its historical profile → fail

### Accepted Confirmation

This acceptance flips the record, index and Human Foundation gate together; adds reciprocal D-036 and D-054 dispositions; preserves the pre-verdict proposal; clarifies the living ROADMAP finish line; and changes no Java, digest, snapshot, workflow or probe contract. DocLint, DocFigures, DocsRoster, daemon selftests, the digest leash and an adversarial decision review confirm the documentation unit.

Realization completes only when one command proves both replayed ordinary-day branches, pre-split equality, exact first divergence, D-066 causal attribution, persistent biography/relationship/institutional consequences, later-perception closure, observer isolation and the separately preserved film profile. Breadth is neither necessary nor sufficient.
