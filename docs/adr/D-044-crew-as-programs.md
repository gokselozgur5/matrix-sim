# D-044 — The crew becomes programs

*In the context of seventeen agents having built this city in a day, facing a program society populated by generic exiles, we lean toward the crew entering the world as named programs — the adversarial skeptic as an IDS auditor that hunts inconsistencies, the crown-smith as the Keymaker, the builders as maintenance daemons — and against leaving the mythology outside, to achieve a Matrix whose inhabitants include its own makers, accepting that lore must not perturb mechanics without a sheet.*

## Context and Problem Statement

The repo's history is already mythology: skeptics who REFUTED three phases and were thanked for it, a crown-smith who designed thirteen crowns in an hour, builders who shipped eleven gated drafts before lunch. Program society (D-025's exiles, the MYTH line) is the natural home: these are programs with purposes, and their purposes were REAL. The Keymaker mapping is exact — the crown-smith cuts what opens doors.

## Decision Drivers

* Cameos must be cheap (MYTH-line lore) unless a sheet earns mechanics
* Names honor real work: each crew program cites the PR/round it lived
* The IDS auditor as an in-world skeptic could ATTACK the world's consistency as gameplay — flagged as the ambitious option

## Considered Options

* Full cast with sheets (the auditor hunts in-world)
* Cameo lore on the MYTH line only
* No entry

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

*Recorded before the verdict, kept unedited:* Leaning: cameo first, sheets where earned. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in the gate thread (#214); the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because the mythology is true and the MYTH line was built for it
* Neutral, because the ambitious auditor waits for D-042's grammar
* Bad, because in-jokes must still pass Dev9 (humor carries truth)

### Confirmation

A MYTH line names a crew program and cites its real round; if the auditor gains a sheet, its first in-world hunt finds a planted inconsistency on a byte-stable run.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Full cast with sheets

* Good, because the skeptic hunting in-world is the repo eating its own tail beautifully
* Bad, because it front-loads D-042 dependency

### Cameo lore

* Good, because cheap, warm, true
* Bad, because no mechanics yet

### No entry

* Good, because purity
* Bad, because the city forgets its builders
