# D-048 — Districts with identity

*In the context of a phase named The Matrix Online that shipped engine breadth, facing the truth that old MxO's breadth was district CHARACTER (Richland's slums vs Downtown's towers), we lean toward districts gaining identity — a character row per zone, instrument-visible, prose-feeding — and against anonymous buckets or a full mechanical redistricting at v1, to achieve a city whose quarters mean something, accepting that any identity touching mechanics is a declared digest move.*

## Context and Problem Statement

The second origin text named the gap: 'adı MxO ama içi engine.' The machinery already waits: PlaceGraph zones carry canonical names (financial district, chinatown, the loop...), RegionMap watches attention per region, and the ATTN/COLD/PARKED ladder produces 'the forgotten neighborhood' as an emergent fact — an unwatched quarter folding into statistics IS urban forgetting, we built it without noticing. Identity turns the fact into a feeling: per-district character, flavor in the instruments, a WHERE with a personality for every log line and every dream-reader paragraph.

## Decision Drivers

* Zones and attention machinery exist — identity is a layer, not a dig
* Lore-first at v1: identity may not touch mechanics until declared (the digest referee rules)
* The dream reader (D-047) and the named cast (D-043) want districts that mean something to walk through
* Old MxO precedent: district character was the world's texture, not a feature

## Considered Options

* Identity as data first (District row: name, character line, prose hooks), mechanics later by declaration
* Identity as mechanics immediately (species mix, commute pull per district)
* Stay anonymous

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

Leaning: data first, mechanics by declaration. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in #223; the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because the city gains texture at zero digest risk
* Good, because every instrument line and prose sentence inherits a WHERE with character
* Bad, because lore-only identity will tempt mechanics smuggling — review discipline

### Confirmation

Each zone carries a character row the instruments and the dream reader quote; a follow-run's prose names its districts in character; canonical digests unchanged until a declared mechanics step.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Data first

* Good, because cheap, honest, immediately felt
* Bad, because identity without consequence risks feeling cosmetic

### Mechanics immediately

* Good, because districts MATTER on day one
* Bad, because undeclared world reshaping at season open — against the layer law

### Anonymous

* Good, because nothing to do
* Bad, because the name debt stands
