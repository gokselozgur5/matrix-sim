# D-066 — Open the Human causal boundary without uploading the mind

*In the context of realizing D-062 and D-065 on the deterministic substrate, facing D-041's identity-derived character layer and sealed-physics rule, we lean toward retaining the four ontological families and physical laws while opening one root-mediated receipt → mind → intent → committed-action → effect boundary, and against either preserving causal insulation or rewriting the universe around mind objects, to make lived interpretation a necessary cause with exhaustive choice/effect provenance, accepting phased ticks, new persistent causal state and complete digest/snapshot/replay coverage.*

## Context and Problem Statement

D-041 correctly diagnosed a finished but characterless miniature. Its layered answer gives names deterministic sheets and lets those sheets vary contests without requiring perception, memory, biography or intention to cause the result. D-062 raises the bar: lived state must be a necessary cause of a reachable choice and persistent future.

The current seams make the tension concrete. `Human` owns identity, a `Brain` whose canonical state is alive/dead, and at most one `NeuralLink`. `PerceptionFrame` queries world facts to render an observer string. Matrix entities tick and queue world mutations without a persistent resident mind mediating them. The package direction is honest, but causal insulation now routes around the subject it protects.

The answer cannot be an `Avatar.brain` field or a `Brain` copied into the Matrix; D-013 exists to forbid exactly that. Nor can an intent become a new direct mutation API. Which inherited laws stay sealed, which D-041 clauses change, and what single grammar may carry Human causality without reversing ownership?

## Decision Drivers

* D-013's no-upload direction, D-012's composition root and D-010's determinism must remain structural laws
* Four ontological families need different vocabularies rather than one behavior skin
* Identity-derived sheets may seed priors and capability but must not substitute for lived belief, memory, biography or choice
* A mind may receive only delivered content, never dereference hidden world truth through provenance
* Intent must remain a proposal until the root validates and canonically commits it
* Every Human-affecting effect needs an exhaustive, mechanically recognizable causal origin
* Causal origin and an affected person's participation/consent role must remain separate
* Every new state bit that can alter a future result must enter digest, snapshot and replay coverage
* Existing runtime evidence and the 152-leaf product backlog need explicit dispositions rather than wholesale inheritance or deletion

## Considered Options

* Keep D-041 unchanged: derived sheets vary behavior above sealed physics
* Rewrite around mind-bearing Matrix entities
* Clause-by-clause amendment: retain ontology and laws, supersede insulation through one root-mediated causal grammar
* Explicit alternative with the same no-upload direction, exhaustive effect attribution and replay obligations

## Decision Outcome

Proposed option: "clause-by-clause amendment with one causal grammar", because the protected object is the ownership and deterministic physics, not the absence of Human causality. Final call in thread #1672 (phase gate).

| D-041 clause | Proposed disposition | Migration consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Four ontological families | KEEP | Humans, programs, machines and systems retain distinct package fire-doors and vocabularies |
| Identity-derived sheets | AMEND | Sheets become deterministic priors/capabilities/temperament; never sufficient belief, biography, relationship, goal or choice |
| Character “on top” of physics | AMEND | Presentation stays on top; lived causal state enters only through the validated action boundary |
| Sealed physics | KEEP laws; SUPERSEDE causal insulation | Space, time, ownership, determinism and ordered mutation remain; committed Human action may cause canonical effects |

The audited 152-leaf corpus partitions into 47 reusable mechanisms, 26 rewrite-required adapters, 44 evidence-only units, 32 parked story units and three obsolete duplicates. Acceptance must retain the exact census and rules rather than turn these totals into a new scheduling quota.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Keep D-041 unchanged

* Good, because every existing physical seam and digest remains untouched
* Neutral, because sheets can continue producing deterministic outcome variety
* Bad, because outcome variety still bypasses persistent situated interpretation
* Bad, because D-062 and D-065 cannot be satisfied by a causal layer forbidden to enter causality

### Rewrite around mind-bearing Matrix entities

* Good, because an entity could read its mind and act locally
* Neutral, because many conventional agent simulations use that ownership
* Bad, because it uploads the mind, reverses D-013 and makes the prison own the person
* Bad, because a rewrite discards validated substrate without proving the replacement

### Clause-by-clause amendment with one causal grammar

* Good, because each old claim keeps or loses authority for an explicit reason
* Good, because immutable records can cross the root while domain ownership stays intact
* Good, because a single action boundary is enumerable and falsifiable
* Bad, because the root must coordinate more phases and causal identities

### Explicit alternative

* Good, because a smaller grammar may be discovered before realization
* Neutral, because record names and package shapes remain open after this decision
* Bad, because any alternative without a complete origin/participation fence merely relocates the bypass

## Accepted Outcome — One causal grammar, one exhaustive fence

The Architect accepted the clause-by-clause amendment in thread #1672 on 2026-08-19 as part of the verdict “üçü de kabul.” This section records that verdict without deleting or rewriting the proposal it answered.

The proposal's D-041 table is accepted as law:

* **KEEP** the four ontological families and their package fire-doors.
* **AMEND** identity-derived sheets into stable deterministic priors, capabilities and temperament only.
* **AMEND** “on top”: presentation remains outside causality, while lived state may enter canonical causality only through the boundary below.
* **KEEP** physical and ownership laws; **SUPERSEDE** the claim that sealing them forbids committed Human action from causing an effect.
* **RETIRE** the old Season Three sequence as current dependency order and classify its leaf corpus by the accepted census.
* **PARK** Architect/Oracle cameos and other story-specific work after the ordinary-day foundation witness.

This preserves D-013's direction. Matrix entity and core packages never receive a `Brain` or another mind object. The mind is not copied, serialized into the Matrix or owned by an avatar. `Simulation` remains the only root above both worlds; explicit identities and immutable causal records cross it.

### The only Human causal path

1. **Truth snapshot.** At canonical tick start, `Simulation` coordinates a frozen view of world and real-side facts eligible for perception. Later mutations cannot leak backward into that tick's delivery.
2. **Delivery audit.** Every attempted delivery records subject identity, tick, channel, actual/declared source when knowable, audit-only truth provenance, fidelity and outcome (`DELIVERED`, `DEGRADED` or `OCCLUDED`). The audit may know the hidden fact and real source. Those fields stay outside the mind-readable receipt projection: calling an identifier opaque is not enough if its value, equality or order can still reveal a hidden distinction.
3. **Percept receipt.** Only delivered or degraded content enters mind input. Its mind-visible projection carries exactly what the subject could perceive, the source as perceived or claimed, uncertainty and a percept identity used only for stable ordering, deduplication and later citation. Actual source and truth provenance remain in a separate root-side audit mapping. A percept identity or its order may not be derived from those hidden fields in a way the mind reducer can observe. Occlusion creates no hidden-fact receipt; a silence/no-signal receipt is allowed only when the silence itself is observable.
4. **Mind transition.** Prior persistent mind state plus one canonically ordered set of mind-visible receipt projections deterministically produces revised meaning, belief, memory and need/value state, then zero or more goal/intent proposals. It cannot query global truth, consult the audit mapping, branch on audit identities or mutate either world.
5. **Intent proposal.** An intent names subject, goal and choice identity, requested act, target and the receipt/memory basis that made it reachable. It is a request, not an effect.
6. **Validation and commitment.** `Simulation` validates current capability, constraints, consent/authority and world truth. It emits either a rejected intent with a reason or one immutable committed action with canonical order, actor choice identity, validating rule and causal identity. A rejection reason is a root-side causal record, not an automatic mind receipt; explaining it later requires a separately audited delivery.
7. **Effect application.** Canonical mutation consumes only a committed action or an explicitly typed non-choice cause event. No behavior, observer, sheet, receipt, prose line or uncommitted intent writes canonical world state directly.
8. **Consequence settlement.** Results update biography, reciprocal relationships, institutions and D-064 breach/repair obligations through named entries. These are future-causal state, not a report assembled after the scene.
9. **Digest and future perception.** Every settled field that may change a future result participates in digest, snapshot and replay. It becomes perceivable only through a later truth snapshot and delivery, closing D-062's loop.

Canonical phase order is:

```text
snapshot truth
  → audit deliveries / issue percept receipts
  → reduce minds
  → propose intents
  → validate / commit
  → apply effects
  → settle biography / relationships / institutions / obligations
  → digest / observe
```

Ordering within each phase is explicit and stable. Randomness, where a later decision permits it, carries a keyed or otherwise reproducible causal stream and provenance; observer order may not consume or reorder domain draws.

**Percept noninterference is law.** Holding prior mind state and the complete ordered mind-visible receipt projections fixed, changing only actual source, hidden truth, audit provenance or audit-only identity must produce byte-identical mind transitions and intent proposals. The audit and later world validation may still differ; the resident's interpretation may not. This covers payload, equality and ordering side channels: hiding the fact while leaking a fact-derived identifier is still a truth bypass.

Percept identity is scoped by Subject and tick. Equal visible occurrences are
not content-deduplicated before allocation: without a visible occurrence
identity, two genuine perceptions cannot be distinguished from a retry.
Allocation preserves their multiplicity, orders only the complete visible
tuple, and assigns dense Subject-local identities. Idempotence begins only
after that point: one exact `(Subject, Percept)` retransmission is one input,
while a conflicting value under the same scoped identity is invalid. The
canonical `MindInput` bytes establish ingress equivalence. Actual reducer
transition equivalence and intent-proposal equivalence remain mandatory gates
on their production leaves; a sample reducer or hand-built intent is not an
end-to-end noninterference proof.

### Java and state boundaries

The decision authorizes categories of change, not a class diagram:

| Existing boundary | Permitted direction |
|---|---|
| `Simulation` | Coordinate the shared phases; translate immutable IDs/records across worlds; validate and canonically order commitments |
| `Human` / `Brain` | Own persistent mind state and biography on the real side; consume only mind-visible receipt projections and produce intents without world or audit queries |
| `PerceptionFrame` | Remain an observer rendering or become an adapter over canonical receipts; its current JSON string cannot serve as hidden mutable mind state |
| `NeuralLink` | Preserve the jack's cross-world association and death law; allow root-mediated immutable receipt/intent transport without exposing a `Brain` to Matrix code |
| `World` / `WorldEvent` | Freeze eligible truth and apply only classified committed/non-choice effects; never hold real-world or mind objects |
| digest / `Snapshot` / `ChronosLog` | Cover every new causal field, pending action and ordering identity needed to reproduce a future result |

Permitted realization therefore includes persistent mind/biography/relationship identity, delivery and receipt grammar, shared tick phases, intent/action/effect records, root-mediated bridges, complete causal-state seals and D-064 settlement.

Still forbidden:

* `entities → realworld` imports or any Matrix object holding a mind reference
* a mind, sheet or behavior querying omniscient truth instead of receipts
* audit-only source, truth, provenance, identity or hidden-derived ordering entering mind input or changing mind reduction
* intent, observer output, prose or a sheet directly mutating canonical state
* a name/sheet threshold standing in for lived interpretation and choice
* causal state or pending order omitted from digest, snapshot or replay
* an observer, probe or follow mode whose presence changes domain bytes or draw order

### Exhaustive choice/effect fence

Every canonical effect involving a Human belongs to exactly one causal-origin class:

* **CHOICE_CAUSED** requires a Human actor, that actor's choice identity, committed-action identity and validating rule. It means the effect originates downstream of that actor's reachable validated choice.
* **NON_CHOICE_CAUSED** has no Human-choice origin and requires a cause event plus subtype: `PHYSIOLOGY`, `ENVIRONMENT`, `NON_HUMAN_ACTOR`, `SYSTEM_IMPOSED` or explicit `UNRESOLVED`. An imposing actor or institution still names initiator and responsible owner.

“Involving a Human” is derived from the state transition, not an author's label. It includes any effect with a Human actor or affected identity and any canonical transition that changes a Human's body/life, perception availability, access/resources, biography, relationship, obligation or future reachable action. `Effect` is the origin-classification surface, not a synonym for phase-seven `WorldEvent`: delivery outcomes and settlement entries remain in the census even when their phase-specific transition occurs elsewhere. Wrapping a Human-affecting transition in a world-only event or another phase does not remove it from the fence.

Every affected Human also carries a participation role separate from causal origin:

* `ACTOR` — the Human whose validated choice is in the origin chain;
* `INFORMED_CONSENTER` — a non-originating affected Human with a cited informed, specific, in-scope and unrevoked consent entry;
* `AFFECTED_WITHOUT_CHOICE` — an affected Human for whom no validated originating choice or qualifying consent is claimed.

Thus one actor's chosen assault is `CHOICE_CAUSED` at origin while the victim is `AFFECTED_WITHOUT_CHOICE`. Causation cannot become the victim's consent or blame. `NON_CHOICE_CAUSED` likewise does not mean harmless or ownerless; D-064 may still assign institutional responsibility and debt.

Mechanical closure:

```text
human_effects = choice_caused + non_choice_caused
choice_caused ∩ non_choice_caused = ∅
unclassified = 0
choice_missing_actor_choice_id = 0
choice_missing_commit_id = 0
choice_missing_validating_rule = 0
non_choice_with_human_choice_origin = 0
affected_humans_missing_participation_role = 0
consent_roles_missing_consent_evidence = 0
hidden_audit_fields_in_mind_input = 0
percept_noninterference_failures = 0
unresolved = 0  # required for the D-065 witness
```

The fence derives the complete effect grammar, every construction/factory site and every handler. A hand-written sample list is not evidence. Adding an effect kind, wrapper or handler without classification turns the gate red.
