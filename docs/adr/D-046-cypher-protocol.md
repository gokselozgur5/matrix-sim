# D-046 — The Cypher protocol

*In the context of a door that only opens outward (opt-out, self-substantiation, jackOut), facing the absence of betrayal as a system force, we lean toward re-insertion as a protocol — voluntary return + memory wipe + a bargain with agents — and against betrayal as scripted drama or nothing, to achieve 'ignorance is bliss' as bookkeeping, accepting that the chronos record will remember what the man forgot.*

## Context and Problem Statement

Cypher is not a character; he is the door's inward direction. Today liberation is one-way and the census only grows or mourns. The protocol: a census member (or a runner mid-world) petitions to return; the system decides what forgetting costs; agents gain a bargaining table (the steak clause — the system PAYS for betrayal, in comfort: a sheet bonus? a protected slot?); the ledger books the re-insertion; the census records a living loss. Deliberate tension flagged rather than hidden: the chronos log remembers the man who forgot — the fold can replay a betrayal its subject no longer knows happened.

## Decision Drivers

* Voluntariness is the point: the protocol needs a WANT (D-042's faith/will axes give it a home)
* The wipe must be real-side state surgery with digest honesty
* The bargain gives agents their first ECONOMY (what do they pay, from what budget?)
* The census's living-loss line must be honest (a mourned man who breathes)

## Considered Options

* The full protocol (petition, wipe, bargain, steak)
* Return without bargain (a door, no drama)
* No inward door

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

*Recorded before the verdict, kept unedited:* Leaning: the full protocol. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in the gate thread (#216); the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because betrayal becomes physics and the door finally swings both ways
* Good, because agents negotiating is new gameplay for the machine family
* Bad, because memory-wipe semantics touch identity-derived sheets (does a wiped man re-derive the same fate? the gate must rule)

### Confirmation

One petition end-to-end on a byte-stable run: the want measured, the bargain logged, the wipe booked, the census line honest, the fold replaying the betrayal identically.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Full protocol

* Good, because ignorance-as-bliss becomes a measurable choice
* Bad, because most moving parts

### Door without bargain

* Good, because simpler
* Bad, because Cypher without the steak is just a U-turn

### No inward door

* Good, because the census stays clean
* Bad, because a one-way door is half a door
