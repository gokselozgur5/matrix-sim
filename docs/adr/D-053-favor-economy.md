# D-053 — The favor economy: orphan insurance

*In the context of the Merovingian graduating from an enum row to a power, facing the temptation of an invented currency, we lean toward favors minted ONLY by sheltering orphans through their grace window — conservation by construction, audited and digested — spent on a small v1 catalog (blind eye, pocket access, papers), and against fiat favors or no economy, to achieve a black market with a conservation law, accepting a single-currency market.*

## Context and Problem Statement

The truce dossier's sharpest mechanism: price the underworld in the only currency the repo ever minted — orphanhood and grace (D-025). The Source collects; an exile shelters the orphan through its grace window; a favor is minted to the shelterer. Favors minted ≡ sheltering events — one conservation law makes it an economy instead of a vibe, FavorAudit carries the proof, the digest carries the balances. The Source's GC pressure becomes the mint's interest rate: the machine's own immune system funds the black market that frustrates it.

## Decision Drivers

* Conservation by construction: the mint has exactly one door
* Priced in existing mechanics (D-025's grace), not invented gold
* Digested balances (I-2: no shadow books)
* The spend catalog starts tiny and grows by record

## Considered Options

* Shelter-minted favors + three-spend v1 catalog
* Fiat favors (orgs print them)
* No economy

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

Leaning: shelter-minted, three spends. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in #229; the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because FavorAudit can prove the whole market honest every run
* Good, because the Merovingian's power is finally mechanical, not titular
* Bad, because a single currency may need a second (information?) later — by record only

### Confirmation

FavorAudit: favors minted ≡ sheltering events across a full run, byte-stable; one favor spent on a patrol's blind eye measurably changes one suppression outcome, logged and replayed.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Shelter-minted

* Good, because conservation is free
* Bad, because mint volume depends on GC pressure tuning

### Fiat

* Good, because easy drama
* Bad, because a printed currency is a vibe with numbers

### None

* Good, because fewer books
* Bad, because the Merovingian stays one enum row forever
