# D-051 — Allegiance and the influence ledger

*In the context of a truce era needing politics, facing the temptation of subclassed factions, we lean toward allegiance as avatar-side data (BLUE/SYMPATHIZER/LOYALIST/CLIENT) whose transitions are seeded exposure events, biting only the acceptance loop's slope, plus a per-district influence three-vector feeding two existing dials, and against faction subclasses or scripted politics, to achieve a replayable cold war with no new physics, accepting that political state is digest-covered state.*

## Context and Problem Statement

The truce dossier's political engine. The three-pole war needs sides without a class explosion (A5/D-015: rows, not hierarchies), conversion without charisma floats (bookkeeping against seeded thresholds), and a battleground that already exists: the anomaly ledger's SLOPE. A SYMPATHIZER's resistance samples positive, a LOYALIST's negative — the D-036 pocket's formula typed at last. The influence ledger is weather, not fate: per-district (Z/M/X), moved by org acts, decaying to equilibrium, read by the Director's awakening cadence and the agents' patrol weight.

## Decision Drivers

* No new physics: politics = the derivative of a number Season One made sacred
* Conversion is bookkeeping — exposure events vs seeded thresholds, replayable by construction
* Influence feeds EXISTING dials (awaken cadence, patrol weight) before it ever grows new ones
* Allegiance is digested state or it does not exist (I-2)

## Considered Options

* Data allegiance + influence three-vector (the dossier's shape)
* Faction subclasses
* No politics — the era stays weatherless

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

*Recorded before the verdict, kept unedited:* Leaning: the dossier's shape. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in #227; the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because the cold war compiles from four enum values and one vector
* Good, because every conversion is a logged, seeded event — the fold replays the politics
* Bad, because allegiance enters the entity digest segment (declared move)

### Confirmation

A recruiter's planted doubt flips one seeded mind SYMPATHIZER (logged, replayed identically); the acceptance loop's slope measurably differs by allegiance (LedgerMirror extended and green); the influence vector moves and decays deterministically under InfluenceAtlas.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Data + vector

* Good, because A5 holds and the war is bookkeeping
* Bad, because vocabulary discipline again (no fifth allegiance without a record)

### Subclasses

* Good, because behavior-per-faction is easy to write
* Bad, because the cast explodes and D-015 dies

### No politics

* Good, because nothing to tune
* Bad, because the era is scenery without sides
