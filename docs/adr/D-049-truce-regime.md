# D-049 — The Truce as a regime

*In the context of a PEACE that is a 900-tick timer between reboots, facing old MxO's lived truth that the Truce was an ERA — a three-pole cold war worked daily by Zion, the Machines, and the Merovingian — we lean toward the Truce as a standing regime (open-door policy, recruiters, a Merovingian power running an exile economy, measurable pole balance) with the overflow arc demoted to the regime's failure mode, and against peace-as-beat, to achieve the untold sixty years as a playable era, accepting that regime mechanics re-tune D-022's economy by declared moves.*

## Context and Problem Statement

The films end at the peace; the only official canon that LIVED past it was MxO. Our D-036 epilogue pocket already parked this era's ingredients ('the untold sixty years... voluntary reinsertion... defector programs'). Today the treaty opens a door once, six walk, a timer runs out, patrols resume. The Merovingian — in MxO a power structure running smuggling, favors, and safe passage — is one row in our ExileKind enum. The regime proposal makes the era the WORLD'S DEFAULT WEATHER and the war its storm.

## Decision Drivers

* The state machine already holds PEACE; an era is policy, not plumbing
* The Merovingian economy gives the program family its first economy (pairs with D-046's agent bargaining)
* Pole balance wants an instrument (a TRUCE line) before it wants mechanics
* The overflow/reboot arc must survive as the failure mode — the film stays reachable

## Considered Options

* The full regime (open-door policy + recruiters + Merovingian economy + balance line)
* Era-lite: standing PEACE + instrument line only
* Peace stays a beat

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

*Recorded before the verdict, kept unedited:* Leaning: era-lite first, the economy as its second act. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in #224; the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because the untold sixty years stop being untold
* Good, because three poles give the character layer a STAGE with sides
* Bad, because a standing era multiplies scenario space — the atlas must learn era verdicts

### Confirmation

A seed can run the Truce as its standing state with the film's arc reachable as its failure; the TRUCE line reports pole balance; the Merovingian's first favor is logged, priced, and replayed identically.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Full regime

* Good, because the era arrives whole
* Bad, because four mechanics land in one verdict

### Era-lite

* Good, because the era exists next week and grows honestly
* Bad, because an era without an economy is scenery at first

### Beat

* Good, because nothing changes
* Bad, because the epilogue pocket stays a pocket
