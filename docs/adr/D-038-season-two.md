# D-038 — Season Two: the epilogue graduates, the project goes public

*In the context of the season-one arc reaching its finish line and the owner asking whether the whole Matrix is really a hundred issues, facing the choice between closing the shop and opening the next act, we decided for a scoped Season Two — the epilogue split into real phases with their own milestones, the repo positioned publicly with releases — and against both unbounded sprawl and premature retirement, to achieve a project that keeps growing with governance intact, accepting that the finish line becomes a season boundary rather than an ending.*

## Context and Problem Statement

D-036 sealed season one: the v3.0 arc is the end of the CORE promise, and the epilogue was a parked pocket. The owner's new commission: position the project, advance it, grow the contribution surface — "is the whole Matrix simulation really just a hundred issues?" It is not. The question is how to grow without dissolving the discipline that got us here.

## Decision Drivers

* The season-one finish line (D-036) must stay meaningful — reached, celebrated, tagged
* Growth must arrive as governed phases, not issue spam: every issue a real unit of work
* Contribution surface: public repo, tagged releases, PR-linked closures (the owner's CP mandate)
* The epilogue ideas deserve film-named homes of their own

## Considered Options

* Season Two: epilogue split into phased milestones; repo public; releases tagged
* Keep the epilogue a single backlog bucket (status quo)
* Unbounded expansion without season boundaries

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Season Two", commissioned by the owner, 2026-08-11. The structure:

- **v4.0 Resurrections** — the Zion era: D-032 fleet + pirate broadcast, D-033 the Kid, the v4 crowns (Zion, Hovercraft, ComputeModel evolution)
- **v4.5 The Second Renaissance** — the history rewrite: D-023 Chronos (event-sourced state, reload = replay)
- **v5.0 The Matrix Online** — the scale era: D-024 attention LOD + D-008 substrate budget + hash-backed hunts + the 5,000-entity budget coming home
- Season one's finish line stands as a **season boundary**: tagged releases v1.0.0…v3.0.0, the completion declared, then season two opens
- Positioning: the repository goes **public**; phase releases carry the lock-report evidence; crowns keep closing via PR keywords

### Consequences

* Good, because the project's growth is again phased, gated and film-named
* Good, because the contribution graph shows real, linked, evidenced work
* Bad, because a public repo means the whole history — including every skeptic refutation — is on display (we consider this a feature; the Architect may consider it marketing)

### Confirmation

Milestones exist for v4.0/v4.5/v5.0 with gates re-homed; releases v1.0.0+ exist with lock-report notes; the repo is public; season-two issues each carry a phase, a label and a real deliverable.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Season Two

* Good, because governance scales with ambition
* Neutral, because the finish line's meaning shifts from "the end" to "the boundary" — D-036's command still gets its green day
* Bad, because more surface to keep true (the standing curation duty grows)

### Status quo backlog bucket

* Good, because zero new structure
* Bad, because a bucket is where ideas go to be forgotten — the owner explicitly asked for more

### Unbounded expansion

* Good, because maximum apparent activity
* Bad, because issue spam is how repos rot; CP built on junk is junk
