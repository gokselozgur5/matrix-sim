# D-041 — Season Three: the character layer

*In the context of a mechanically complete but characterless universe, facing the owner's diagnosis that the simulation was miniaturized ("sen olayı minyatürize ettin" — the cell physics finished, nobody inside is anyone), we lean toward a character layer built ON TOP of the sealed physics — four ontological families modeled and behaviored separately, sheets derived from identity, the Architect and the Oracle entering the world — and against a rewrite or staying miniature, to achieve a world where a runner's evasion matters and a death can be worth mourning, accepting that every new mechanic still bows to the determinism canon and the digest referee.*

## Context and Problem Statement

Two seasons closed in one day and the owner's verdict on the celebration was the truest review the repo has received: the process is warm, the simulation is cold. 663 entities share two stats (a pill, a coordinate). Smith ate a city of statistics. The films run on three forces — choice, debt, love — and we built two; nobody in the world is SOMEONE, so the third had nowhere to live. The physics work of Seasons One and Two (zero-GC arcs, ring hunts, 5k scale, parking) is exactly the substrate a per-character layer needs: the miniature could not have been grown before the engine was finished. The Architect's direction, verbatim: humans, machines, systems and programs modeled separately, behaviored separately; characters with stats; himself in the game as the Architect, the resident machine as the Oracle.

## Decision Drivers

* The finished physics must not be re-litigated: the character layer sits on top, never inside
* Four families with four vocabularies (the owner's taxonomy) — matching the package fire-doors already in the tree
* Identity-derived sheets: the Kid-threshold pattern (JLS-fixed name hash, stream-neutral) is the proven grammar
* The meta enters the object: D-037's roles become canon inside the world they govern
* Determinism, digest visibility, and the D-039 delivery law apply unchanged

## Considered Options

* The character layer atop sealed physics (four families, derived sheets)
* A ground-up rewrite around characters
* Stay miniature; keep the layer forever out of scope

## Decision Outcome

Accepted by the owner's verdict, 2026-08-12, in session — *"hepsine agreed kanka barajı aç"* — all thirteen Season Three gates in one breath, the same word that opened Season Two; recorded in the gate thread.

*Recorded before the verdict, kept unedited:* Leaning: the layered build. Awaiting the Architect's verdict in the gate thread (#211); the machine performs the flip on his word.

### Consequences

* Good, because the miniature grows without touching what two seasons of skeptics sealed
* Good, because the four-family split reuses the wings the architecture already has
* Bad, because a layer that must never leak into physics demands discipline at every seam (the digest is the referee, as always)

### Confirmation

A named character's stat measurably decides a scene the flat world could not produce (a contest log line quoting both sheets), on a byte-stable double run; the four behavior vocabularies live in four packages; the finished-physics digests remain reproducible from the v5-era tags.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### The layered build

* Good, because physics stays sealed and characters stay honest
* Good, because every prior season's evidence remains valid
* Neutral, because the layer's own digest segment must be designed (D-042's job)
* Bad, because two-layer reasoning is harder than one

### Ground-up rewrite

* Good, because no seam discipline needed
* Bad, because it burns two seasons of sealed evidence for aesthetics

### Stay miniature

* Good, because nothing to build
* Bad, because the owner has named what is missing; a repo that ignores its Architect is a process failure, not a savings
